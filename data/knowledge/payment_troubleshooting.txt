Payment Troubleshooting Knowledge

Payment errors:
- Payment failures can occur when payment requests cannot be completed successfully.
- Repeated payment errors may indicate problems with a payment dependency or service.
- A high number of payment errors within a short time window can indicate an abnormal condition.

Common payment problems:
- Payment request failures.
- Payment service unavailable.
- Dependency failures.
- Request timeouts.
- High error rates.

Investigation steps:
1. Check the payment error rate.
2. Check whether payment requests are timing out.
3. Check dependent payment services.
4. Check whether failures are affecting multiple requests.
5. Review recent payment service logs.

Recommended actions:
- Investigate the failing payment requests.
- Check payment service availability.
- Check dependent services.
- Review recent error logs.
- Monitor the payment error rate after applying a fix.
